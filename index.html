Four problems we want to solve

Fragmented ESG data

ESG information sits in separate Excel files, utility bills, waste records, employee reports and production databases, which makes one consistent report hard to build.

Time-consuming manual reporting

Employees repeatedly copy information, check figures, calculate indicators and prepare reports, leaving less time for analysis.

Data quality and confidentiality risks

Missing values, inconsistent units, duplicates and incorrect figures hurt reliability, and businesses worry about exposing sensitive information.

Limited performance visibility

It is hard to compare ESG performance across months, factories, departments or reporting periods.

These are hypotheses from our research plan. We will test them in customer interviews and do not assume every company has the same problems.

How it works

1

Customer data sources

Customers select relevant records: Excel files, electricity bills, production reports, waste records and approved business-system data.

2

Customer-authorized transfer

Customers upload files or authorize supported connections. Defined permissions and access controls apply.

3

ESG AI platform

The system processes the authorized data, checks data quality, calculates selected indicators and analyzes performance.

4

Dashboard and draft report

Dashboards, trend comparisons, missing-data warnings, calculation references and editable report drafts.

5

Human review and approval

Company representatives review results, correct errors and approve the report before sharing or publishing it.

The prototype

Our first prototype is spreadsheet-based and uses simulated data unless a business provides authorized information.

1. Data upload

Excel or CSV with monthly electricity, production and waste data.

2. Data validation

Missing values, invalid entries, duplicates and inconsistent units. A blank is treated as unknown, never zero.

3. KPI dashboard

Selected indicators and monthly trends in interactive charts.

4. AI-assisted report

An editable draft with approved data, charts and explanations.

5. Review and approval

Users check calculations, correct errors and mark the report reviewed or approved.

Example indicators

IndicatorExample calculation
Energy intensityElectricity consumed ÷ units produced
Carbon emissionsActivity data × applicable emissions factor
Recycling rateRecycled waste ÷ total waste × 100%

Carbon emissions need an emissions factor for the relevant location, period and activity. The platform keeps the factor's source and method so results can be checked.

Privacy and security by design

What we aim to do

  • Customers choose which data they upload or connect
  • Only the information needed for the agreed service is collected
  • Role-based access permissions
  • Important access and data changes are recorded
  • Data is protected during transfer and storage
  • Customer information is not used to train AI models unless explicitly agreed and authorized
  • Customer review is required before reports are shared externally

Legal context

Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) is relevant when identifiable personal data is processed. Commercially confidential business information also needs appropriate technical and contractual protection, even when it is not personal data.

ESG AutoPilot AI assists ESG management and reporting. It does not independently certify ESG performance or guarantee regulatory compliance.
